About us

A nursery that grew
into a way of living.

At RK Nursery & Sons, our passion for greenery and elegant living spaces drives everything we do. We believe every home deserves a touch of nature, and our mission is to transform balconies, terraces, gardens, and indoor spaces into beautiful, refreshing environments through thoughtfully curated plants, premium planters, and professional landscaping services.

Based in Punjabi Bagh, Delhi, RK Nursery & Sons offers an extensive collection of premium Indian and imported pots, decorative planters, indoor and outdoor plants, and customized landscaping services. Every product and service is thoughtfully selected to enhance both aesthetics and functionality, ensuring a perfect balance of style and nature.

At RK Nursery & Sons, we believe that greenery is more than just decoration — it is a way of enriching everyday life. Every plant we nurture, every planter we curate, and every space we transform reflects our commitment to quality, craftsmanship, and customer satisfaction.
